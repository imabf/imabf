## Steps to reproduce
1. Open the login page.
2. Enter a valid username with a wrong password and click Login. Repeat 10 times.
3. Enter the correct password.

## Expected result
After a set number of failures the account is locked or attempts are throttled (for example a delay or CAPTCHA).

## Actual result
Every attempt is processed normally; the correct password still works after many failures.

## Impact
Allows unlimited password guessing (brute force).
